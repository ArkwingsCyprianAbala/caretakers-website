About Our School

A Place Where Every Child Thrives

Located in the heart of Kihunguro, Kikuyu Town, Caretakers' Center of Excellence has been nurturing young minds since 2021. We believe every child is naturally curious and capable — our Montessori-trained teachers create a warm, structured environment where children learn at their own pace.

🎓

Authentic Montessori Method

Internationally recognized approach with hands-on materials that build independence, focus, and a love for learning.

❤️

Nurturing, Safe Environment

Your child's safety and emotional wellbeing are our top priority. We build confidence alongside academic skills.

👨‍👩‍👧

Strong Parent Partnership

We keep parents informed and involved at every step of their child's journey.

Our Programmes

Learning Tailored to Every Stage

Four carefully structured programmes, each designed to meet your child exactly where they are and prepare them for the next exciting stage.

🐣 Ages 2 – 3 Years

Day Care

A gentle, loving introduction to school life. Toddlers thrive in a safe, stimulating environment that nurtures their earliest development.

  • Sensory exploration
  • Early language skills
  • Practical life routines
  • Social & emotional growth
Most Enrolled 🌱 Ages 3 – 4 Years

Pre-Primary 1

Children begin structured Montessori learning — developing early literacy, numeracy, and independence through hands-on discovery.

  • Phonics & early reading
  • Number recognition
  • Montessori materials
  • Creative arts & music
🌟 Ages 4 – 5 Years

Pre-Primary 2

Building on Pre-Primary 1, children deepen their reading, writing, and mathematical thinking in a rich Montessori environment.

  • Reading & writing fluency
  • Early mathematics
  • Science & nature
  • Problem solving
🚀 Ages 5 – 6 Years

Kindergarten (KG)

Our top programme builds confident, well-rounded children who are fully prepared for the next chapter — academically strong and emotionally ready.

  • Advanced reading & writing
  • Number operations
  • Critical thinking
  • Full school readiness
