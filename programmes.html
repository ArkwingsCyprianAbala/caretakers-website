Our Programmes

Learning Tailored to Every Stage

Four carefully structured programmes, each designed to meet your child exactly where they are and prepare them for the next exciting stage.

🐣 Ages 2 – 3 Years

Day Care

A gentle, loving introduction to school life. Toddlers thrive in a safe, stimulating environment that nurtures their earliest development.

  • Sensory exploration
  • Early language skills
  • Practical life routines
  • Social & emotional growth
Most Enrolled 🌱 Ages 3 – 4 Years

Pre-Primary 1

Children begin structured Montessori learning — developing early literacy, numeracy, and independence through hands-on discovery.

  • Phonics & early reading
  • Number recognition
  • Montessori materials
  • Creative arts & music
🌟 Ages 4 – 5 Years

Pre-Primary 2

Building on Pre-Primary 1, children deepen their reading, writing, and mathematical thinking in a rich Montessori environment.

  • Reading & writing fluency
  • Early mathematics
  • Science & nature
  • Problem solving
🚀 Ages 5 – 6 Years

Kindergarten (KG)

Our top programme builds confident, well-rounded children who are fully prepared for the next chapter — academically strong and emotionally ready.

  • Advanced reading & writing
  • Number operations
  • Critical thinking
  • Full school readiness
